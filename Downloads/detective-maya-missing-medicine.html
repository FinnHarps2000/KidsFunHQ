The Case of the Missing Medicine

Dr Chen arrived at Paws & Claws Animal Hospital this morning to find her special animal medicine — gone! Without it, the poorly patients can't be treated. The waiting room is full of nervous owners and one very suspicious-looking parrot. Maya has been called in. She is slightly concerned there will be animals. There will definitely be maths.

🏥
Case Closed!
🧒‍♀️
Chester was given a formal caution and had his vitamin banana treats reduced to two per day. He is not pleased about this, but Dr Chen points out that 2 × 7 days per week is still 14 banana treats. Maya calculated that for him. He perked up slightly. Then stole another vitamin. Maya pretended not to see.
Maya walked out into the car park feeling like a proper mathematician.

"Times tables aren't hard facts to memorise — they're patterns. Double-double for ×4. Count in 5s for ×5. Five-plus-one for ×6. Once you see the pattern, the maths just... works."

She checked her notepad. Five cases. Five clues. Ten multiplication facts. All solved. Not bad for a morning at the vet.
