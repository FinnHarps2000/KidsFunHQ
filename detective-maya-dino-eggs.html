Leftover Cookies!

The café chef baked cookies and tried to put them in boxes, but there were some left over. Maya can tell the eggs came through here because of the crumbs! Solve the sharing puzzle to find the clue under the right table.

🧮 22 cookies shared into groups of 5. How many full groups? How many left over?
🌟 Sometimes things don't share perfectly!
1
Put 5 cookies into each group. Keep going until you don't have enough for a full group.
2
We can fill 4 full groups of 5 (that's 20 cookies). We have 2 left over. The leftover is called the remainder!
3
We write this as: 22 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 2. Check: (4 × 5) + 2 = 20 + 2 = 22 ✅
4
To find remainder: multiply the answer by the divisor, subtract from the original. 22 - 20 = 2!

🎯 Now try: 17 ÷ 3 = ? remainder ?
🌟 The checklist method
1
How many 3s fit in 17? Count up: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 — that is 5 groups. (6 groups would be 18, too many!)
2
5 groups of 3 = 15. Subtract: 17 - 15 = 2. So remainder is 2!
3
Check: (5 × 3) + 2 = 15 + 2 = 17 ✅
Count how many full groups fit: for 22 ÷ 5, count 5, 10, 15, 20 — that is 4 groups. 22 - 20 = 2 left over (remainder 2).
