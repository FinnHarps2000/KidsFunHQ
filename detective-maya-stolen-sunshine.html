The Caretaker's Shed

The thief left the Solar Crystal hidden inside a special box in the shed. The caretaker's note says: "The box has 6 flat faces, 12 edges, and 8 corners. All faces are rectangles."

Maya must identify the correct 3D shape to open the right box!

🔷 Which shape has 6 faces, 12 edges and 8 corners — and all faces are rectangles?
🔺
Cone
1 flat face, 1 curved face
🔵

📦

6 faces, 12 edges, 8 corners
🔺
Pyramid
5 faces, 8 edges, 5 corners
🥫
Cylinder

🎲
Cube
6 equal square faces
🎯 Bonus! A triangular prism has how many faces?
Think about a cereal box or a brick. Count its flat sides — that is a face! A cube has 6 equal faces, but a rectangular prism can have faces that are different sizes.
