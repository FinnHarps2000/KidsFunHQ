🎭 The School Hall — Clue 4 of 5
🎭
The Cake Evidence!
🧒‍♀️
In the middle of the hall — the volcano cake! Or what is left of it. Someone has eaten part of it and left the rest on a chair. They ate the evidence. That is genuinely the worst plan I have ever seen.
🧒‍♀️
The cake was cut into 8 equal slices. Some slices are missing. To work out who was here, I need to figure out the fractions — that means what part of the whole cake is gone!
🎂 The cake had 8 slices. 3 slices are missing (eaten!). What fraction of the cake is gone?
🌟 Fractions show parts of a whole!
1
The cake is cut into 8 equal slices. The bottom number of a fraction is always how many equal parts the whole is cut into. So the bottom number is 8.
2
The top number shows how many parts you are talking about. 3 slices are missing, so the top number is 3.
3
So 3 out of 8 slices are gone. We write that as 3/8 — "three eighths". Three out of eight!
🧒‍♀️
Maya finds a crumpled receipt on the floor. Someone bought kitchen supplies from the staffroom. The receipt is split into fractions to make it confusing. Whoever did this clearly studied villaining instead of maths.
🧩 There are 12 biscuits left on a plate. The thief ate 1/4 of them. How many did they eat? (1/4 of 12 = ?)
🌟 "Of" means divide!
1
1/4 of 12 means divide 12 into 4 equal groups. How many in each group?
2
12 ÷ 4 = 3. So 1/4 of 12 = 3. Three biscuits per group, and we want 1 group!
For fractions: the bottom number is the total pieces. The top is your share. For "1/4 of 12": divide 12 into 4 equal groups — 12 ÷ 4 = 3!
